## Core loop

```
DAY:  calls → gear check → training missions → earn $/XP
NIGHT: dispatch calls → some are ANOMALIES → co-op contain/survive → catalog + bonus
META: prestige nights 7/14/30 · weekly anomaly · cosmetics
```

---

## +20% differentiation

**Anomalies = reality glitches**, not monsters:

- Hydrant «wrong» (no pressure)
- Building map shifts between dispatch and arrival
- Radio whispers false coords
- Fire burns but doesn't heat (trap)
- NPC stands still in smoke

**Not:** another firefighter RP sim.

---

## Co-op roles (2–4)

| Роль | Job |
|---|---|
| **Engine** | Hose, breach, extinguish zones, carry NPC |
| **Dispatch** | Station cameras, radio, mark «suspicious call» |
| **Rescue** | Evacuation, stretcher, countdown |
| **Security** (VIP cosmetic) | Wide flashlight skin, emotes — **no stun paywall** |

One player **cannot** complete night alone — info asymmetry like Animal Hospital.

---

## MVP 2 weeks

**Map:**
- 1 fire station (hub)
- 1 residential building (mission zone)

**Modes:** day/night same world

**3 anomaly types:**

1. **False Alarm** — fake call wastes time/$
2. **Smoke That Lies** — visible smoke, no real fire (trap)
3. **Backdraft Blink** — timed flashover zone, must retreat/seal

**Out of MVP:**
- Realistic fire spread simulation
- City open world
- Fleet of vehicles
- Complex NPC AI

---

## Fire MVP tech (solo-friendly)

- Fire = **zones** (burning/smoldering/out), not fluid sim
- Smoke = fog + debuff, not heavy physics
- NPC = static rescue targets + simple carry
- Extraction timer = main tension driver
