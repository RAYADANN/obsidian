# 7 паттернов multi-hit девов

> Часть: [[00 — оглавление]] · [[Как топ-девы выбирают идеи — обзор]]

---

## 1. Ищут signal, не «придумывают hit»

- Jandel: broken game + 5k CCU = potential
- Voldex/Do Big: buy traction
- Wonder Works: 1M favorites **before** mega launch

---

## 2. Backlog годами

Nathan + Jandel: **~200 ideas** on shelf, refine 1–3 years before commit.

---

## 3. Kill fast — sacred rule

> *«Worked 6 months and no one's playing? Just ditch it.»* — Jandel

Grandma's Favourite: *Know When to Move On*

---

## 4. Differentiation, not «better clone»

Nathan (Naavik):
> First-mover advantage is **insurmountable**.  
> Can't «make that game but better».

Must be **significantly different** — by then you're not competing, you're adjacent genre.

---

## 5. Distribution designed in

| Studio | Tactic |
|---|---|
| Wonder Works | 3–4 influencer videos per update |
| SpyderSammy | Admin Abuse = content strategy |
| Grandma's Favourite | «Envision YouTubers playing it» |
| Zach Letter | Scarcity/FOMO → trading content |

Paid influencer promos: **USELESS** if not organic (Nathan).

---

## 6. Brutal scope

| Example | Scope |
|---|---|
| 99 Nights | 3 months (vs 6 before) |
| Twin Atlas rapid | 10 games/year, 1 month each |
| Wonder Works | 4–6 week cycles |
| SAB | 4 months total |

---

## 7. Два пути к portfolio success

- **Many shots:** prototype division, franchise iteration (Preston, Nathan)
- **Acquire scale:** Voldex, Do Big, Jandel on Dusty Trip / Grow a Garden

---

## Что объединяет vs Deep Digger-style fail

| Multi-hit pattern | Typical sim fail |
|---|---|
| Prototype → metrics | Polish 6 mo → launch |
| Weekly live ops | Set and forget |
| Clip / social stakes | Solo grind |
| Trend or gap | Generic genre |
| Kill rule | Sunk cost fallacy |
