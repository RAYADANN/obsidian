## Steal a Brainrot — не «магическая формула», а checklist

### Что сработало (не abstract fun)

| Фактор | Детали |
|---|---|
| **Trend timing** | Italian brainrot peak TikTok early 2025 |
| **Existing loop** | Купили Steal a Character, улучшили |
| **One twist** | PvP steal на collection/tycoon |
| **Pre-built IP** | Kids already know characters |
| **Clip engine** | Kids crying when stolen → TikTok |
| **Dev-as-content** | Admin Abuse weekly |
| **Speed** | ~4 months to launch |

### Чего НЕ было
- Оригинального deep lore
- «Перепроходи историю»
- Polish AAA

## Что top devs НЕ говорят как «формула fun»

Они говорят:
1. **Signal first** (prototype CCU, opt-in, D1)
2. **Trend window** (meme rising, not dying)
3. **Clip test** ( understandable in 15 sec video)
4. **One twist** on proven genre
5. **Kill fast** if no players

---

## Brainrot 2026 status

- Genre **permanent** (~1.4M CCU sustained)
- **Pure steal/escape** — saturating / declining
- **Brainrot + other mechanic** — still upside per Market Friday

## Anti-patterns

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Escape tsunami clone | −78% CCU week-over-week |
| Story campaign core | One-and-done |
| «Better SAB» | First-mover + community moat |
| Launch without weekly ops | RFY 28d window |
