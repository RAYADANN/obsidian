## Стратегия «brainrot as skin»

Brainrot = **permanent genre** (не meme spike).  
Играть не «ещё один pure brainrot», а:

```
Brainrot theme + underserved mechanic
  → Brainrot Fighting
  → Brainrot TD
  → Brainrot Town/City
```

Borrow discovery, fight in less crowded mechanical space.
