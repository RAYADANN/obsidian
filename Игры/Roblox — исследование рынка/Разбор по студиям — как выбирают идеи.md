## Voldex — Acquire + Scale

|---|---|

## Nathan Clemens / Simple Games — Portfolio Shooter

**24+ games**, UndoneBuilder brand.

- Rapid prototypes, genre experiments
- **Kill:** no audience when dev cycle ends
- Differentiation mandatory — never «better clone»
- Paid YouTuber promos useless without organic

---

## Twin Atlas — Original IP Factory

- ~10 games/year, ~1 month each
- Separate divisions per prototype
- Brands: SpongeBob TD, etc.
- Signal from internal playtests → soft launch

---

## Wonder Works — Licensed IP + Influencer Native

**CEO Zach Letter**

- Gap in genre + **IP kids know**
- 1M favorites before mega push
- 3–4 creator videos per update
- 4–6 week update cycles
- Scarcity/FOMO for trading content

---

## Grandma's Favourite — Game Jam → Breakout

**99 Nights in the Forest**

- 1 week jam → 3 month polish (vs 6 before)
- «Envision YouTubers playing it»
- Co-op survive + collection, not story replay
- Know when to move on (kill rule)

---

## Gamer Robot — Single IP Longevity

**Blox Fruits** — one game, years of updates, anime trend riding.

Not portfolio — **depth on one hit**.

---

## Nikilis — Solo One-IP

**Murder Mystery 2** — 10+ years, social deduction, seasonal content.

Idea = **one strong loop**, not many shots.
