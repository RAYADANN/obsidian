# Механика — Idle / Offline progress

> Часть: [[00 — оглавление]] · Meta: **M4** · Engine: **E1**

## Суть
Прогресс **продолжается без онлайна**. Login = «welcome back, you earned X».

## Core verb
`wait` · `accumulate` · `collect`

## Что цепляет
- **Welcome back gift** — pile of rewards on login
- **«It grew while I slept»** — satisfying return

| Hook сила | ★★★ (return hook, not first visit) |

## Что удерживает
- **Primary RETURN driver** for E1 games
- **Habit loop** — check every few hours
- **Cap + upgrade** — earn faster offline

| D1 | D7 | D28 |
|---|---|---|
| ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★ |

## Design rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Cap offline earnings** | Prevent infinite AFK meta |
| **Show timer** | «Full in 4h 23m» |
| **Boost online play** | Online > offline (optional) |
| **Collect button** | Satisfying claim moment |

## Roblox примеры

GaG (crops grow), Brainrot ($/sec on base), Pet Sim (auto-farm pets).

## Solo dev

| | |
|---|---|
| **Complexity** | Low |
| **MVP** | Timestamp save + calculate on join |
| **2026** | E1 alone = 🔴 — need unique noun |

## Лучшие comбо

| Partner | Why |
|---|---|
| Collection | Hatch while away |
| Events | «Double offline weekend» |
| Steal | Fear someone takes offline earnings |
| Rebirth | Faster offline rate per tier |

## Anti-patterns

- Offline = only progression (no active loop)
- 24h offline cap with no online hook
- «Garden clone» in 2026
